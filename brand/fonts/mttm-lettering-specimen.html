Details

The O is a true circle, as in the logo. The zero is a rounded square so 0 and O never get confused. I is a single stroke, and W is the M turned upside down.

How it works

  • The brand spacing is built in: one letter-width between characters and three between words, with no letter-spacing needed. Leave letter-spacing at 0.
  • Capitals only. Lowercase keys type capitals.
  • Use it for headlines, labels, title cards, highlight text and merch slogans. Keep lines short. Use Manrope for anything read as sentences.
  • Heavy is for small sizes (labels under about 14 px high on screen) and embroidery.
  • The logo stays fixed artwork. Never retype it, even though the font has the letters.
