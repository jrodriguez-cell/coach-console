1 : 1 VS 1.25 : 1

The same lettering, spacing rules, monogram rules and colours. Only the letter width changes.

Recommendation: 1 : 1. It is the only version where the O is the true circle you asked for, where the spacing is the same unit in every direction, and where the monogram is a perfect square. It is also the stronger performer where the brand appears most: the avatar, highlights, hats and small sizes.

Choose 1.25 if, when you look at the wordmark rows below, the slightly extended, more Michroma-like feel matters more to you than the circular O and the bigger small-size letters.
