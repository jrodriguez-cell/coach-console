Round 5 · centred · circular O · plain I · simple K

What changed from your feedback

  • Layout A, centred is now the only layout.
  • O is circular: a true circle (or ellipse) instead of a rounded rectangle.
  • I is plain: a single stroke with no top or bottom bars.
  • K is simpler: two straight diagonals meeting the stem, with no stub.
  • Letters are less wide: because every letter shares one width, the O can only be a true circle when the letters are square. So my pick is 1 : 1. Two slightly wider steps are shown too.
  • Unchanged: thin Michroma-weight strokes, equal letter widths and heights, and the spacing rule: a gap of one letter width between letters and one letter height between lines, with the monogram on the same gaps. At 1 : 1 the gaps are the same in both directions, so the monogram is a perfect square.
Last check: pick a proportion (1 : 1, 1.15 or 1.3). If it looks right, I’ll build the full set next: final logos, PNG exports, Instagram avatar, highlights, interview and Reel templates, and the guidelines.
SPACING RULE · letter = W × H · gap between letters = W · gap between lines = H · monogram uses the same gaps
At 1 : 1 → one unit everywhere: letters 1 × 1, 1 apart, lines 1 apart.
